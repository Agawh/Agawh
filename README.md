### 🌙 Flagship: Alune

> *Unified Retail and Point of Sale Ecosystem*

▸ **Alune POS · Web and PWA**  
Touch-first checkout engineered for high retail throughput. Delivers instant barcode scan lookups, thermal receipts, real-time USD and Bs conversions, and exact balance reconciliations for cash drawers.  
`React 19` · `TypeScript` · `Vite` · `Tailwind CSS` · `shadcn/ui` · `Supabase` · `Cloudflare`

▸ **Alune Core · Backend and Inventory Engine**  
The operational heart of the system. Manages multi-store inventory, supplier purchasing, and an immutable Kardex ledger providing audit history and dependable stock tracking across locations.  
`Python` · `SQLite` · `Pytest`
